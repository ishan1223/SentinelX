Innovation 01
Per-Host Z-Score Baselines
Converts 9-dimensional raw metrics into host-normalized statistical z-scores with dynamic variance floors. Solves device role asymmetry so firewalls and quiet workstations are judged strictly against their own baselines.
Innovation 02
Multi-Signal Noise Filter Gate
Suppresses false alarms by requiring at least 2 independent signal deviations (|z| ≥ 2.0) across DNS, outbound bytes, process creation, and failed logins before escalating compromise probability.
Innovation 03
Deterministic Explainability
Replaces unexplainable black-box AI with exact mathematical contribution percentages (summing to 100%) and actionable, non-destructive response playbooks for SOC tier-1 analysts.
