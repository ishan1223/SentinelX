# SENTINELX — ULTRA-SHORT 10-SECOND ANSWERS
**Usage:** When judges interrupt or demand instant, punchy answers  

---

1. **Judge:** "What's your backend?"  
   **Answer:** "FastAPI on Python 3.12 with SQLite 3, handling async telemetry ingestion, ML inference, and 11 REST API endpoints."

2. **Judge:** "What's your ML model?"  
   **Answer:** "An unsupervised Isolation Forest with 200 decision trees scoring per-host z-score normalized feature vectors."

3. **Judge:** "Where's your data?"  
   **Answer:** "Synthetically generated in software modeling 5 host roles using Poisson and Gaussian distributions."

4. **Judge:** "How accurate?"  
   **Answer:** "It is an unsupervised model; we verify statistical baseline separation and zero false alarms across 53 automated unit tests."

5. **Judge:** "What's innovative?"  
   **Answer:** "Our two-stage pipeline: per-host z-score normalization to solve device asymmetry, plus a multi-signal noise gate to eliminate single-metric false alarms."

6. **Judge:** "Where does inference happen?"  
   **Answer:** "In-memory inside `AnomalyDetectionService.score()` taking under 1.2 milliseconds per sample."

7. **Judge:** "How is risk calculated?"  
   **Answer:** "$0.50 \times \text{ML Anomaly Score} + 0.30 \times \text{Correlation Breadth} + 0.20 \times \text{Severity Factor}$."

8. **Judge:** "What triggers an incident?"  
   **Answer:** "Compromise probability $\ge 80.0\%$ combined with an active ticket deduplication check."

9. **Judge:** "How do you handle false positives?"  
   **Answer:** "Our noise gate requires at least 2 independent signals with $|z| \ge 2.0$ before boosting risk."

10. **Judge:** "Why not PostgreSQL?"  
    **Answer:** "SQLite provided zero-dependency in-process simplicity for the prototype; PostgreSQL with TimescaleDB is our production target."

11. **Judge:** "How do you prevent SQL injection?"  
    **Answer:** "All queries in `database.py` use parameterized SQL binding with positional and named placeholders."

12. **Judge:** "What happens if a feature has zero variance?"  
    **Answer:** "We enforce a dynamic standard deviation floor of $\max(0.5, 0.02 \times |\mu|)$ to prevent zero division."

13. **Judge:** "How does the frontend get data?"  
    **Answer:** "Axios HTTP client polling our FastAPI REST endpoints every 7 seconds."

14. **Judge:** "How many endpoints are in your demo?"  
    **Answer:** "5 simulated devices: 3 workstations, 1 perimeter firewall, and 1 core router."

15. **Judge:** "What happens when you click 'Simulate Attack'?"  
    **Answer:** "`POST /api/simulation/compromise` skews 6 telemetry signals on HOST-042, triggering an automated incident ticket."

16. **Judge:** "How do you handle concept drift?"  
    **Answer:** "The background loop retrains the Isolation Forest every 30 ticks on all confirmed normal historical data."

17. **Judge:** "How would you scale to 10,000 endpoints?"  
    **Answer:** "Decouple ingestion with Apache Kafka, cache baselines in Redis, and persist in TimescaleDB."

18. **Judge:** "Is your explainability generated by an LLM?"  
    **Answer:** "No, it is deterministic: it computes exact percentage contribution shares from z-scores to ensure verifiable auditability."

19. **Judge:** "Can an incident ticket be reopened?"  
    **Answer:** "Yes, once an incident is marked `RESOLVED`, any new compromise crossing 80% creates a new ticket."

20. **Judge:** "What is your biggest current limitation?"  
    **Answer:** "Operating on synthetic telemetry streams rather than live eBPF kernel network collectors."
